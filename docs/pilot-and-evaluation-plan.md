# Pilot and evaluation plan

## Pilot bands

| Band | Primary question | First intervention bias |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 600–640 | Can the learner identify and execute a basic method with support? | Explicit teaching, bilingual explanation, worked example, blocked practice. |
| 650–690 | Can the learner apply a repeatable procedure reliably? | Question-type recognition, rule retrieval, distractor analysis, fading support. |
| 700–740 | Can the learner transfer and discriminate under changed surface forms? | Interleaving, contrastive distractors, efficiency, strategy selection. |
| 750–800 | Can the learner reduce variance and fragile precision failures? | Near-misses, timing, confidence calibration, fatigue, rare traps, score-floor stability. |

## Pilot sequence

1. Onboard with language, separate Reading and Writing/Math context, test date, and a provisional band.
2. Record a manual official assessment summary without copying content.
3. Run a synthetic or user-described mistake autopsy.
4. Assign a 20-minute sprint and record purpose, result, confidence, and fatigue.
5. Revisit after a delay with near or far transfer as evidence allows.
6. Run a strategy trial only when comparable items and a defined outcome exist.
7. Reassess on unused official SAT/Bluebook material where authorized.

## Evaluation measures

Do not rely on immediate generated-question accuracy. Track:

- later official score and domain indicators;
- repeated error-signature recurrence and resolution;
- near and far transfer on unused material;
- timing, omissions, answer changes, and end-of-module effects;
- confidence calibration and overconfidence frequency;
- score variance and score floor across comparable assessments;
- adherence to the 20-minute plan and learner-reported workload;
- whether interventions are smaller and more targeted than undirected question volume.

## Evidence standards

Separate product instrumentation from efficacy claims. A local prototype can show that it stored a hypothesis, scheduled a review, or enforced a 20-minute plan. It cannot show score improvement, question quality, browser compatibility, or official transfer until those are independently measured with appropriate material and consent.

## Stop conditions

Pause a pilot if the workflow begins collecting credentials, persists official content, claims an official score from simulated items, exceeds the intended time budget, or assigns a confirmed diagnosis from one event.
